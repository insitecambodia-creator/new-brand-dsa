## Principles

- Avoid thin pages created only to match the hierarchy; merge into overviews until content justifies a page.
- Dental: add-on positioning only; no standalone individual programme claims, no "No Medical Plan Required", no unverified prices.
- Article topics (provisional; assign from real content, not slugs): Health & Medical; Maternity & Family; Life & Income Protection; Home, Lifestyle & Travel; Living in Singapore; Employee Benefits & Wellbeing; Business & Cyber Risks; Company News.
- Review-before-migration items (brief section 5) stay `unresolved` in the inventory until decided; no removal or merger is approved.
